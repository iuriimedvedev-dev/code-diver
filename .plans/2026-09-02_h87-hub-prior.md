## Hypothesis

WHERE-style queries ("where is X implemented/managed/coordinated") target **hub files**. Hubness is
measurable without query-specific tuning:
1. **Fan-in centrality** from the JVM graph artifact (`GraphNeighborIndex` incoming adjacency) — principled, generic.
2. **Filename-role prior** — hub suffixes (`Manager`, `Processor`, `Service`, `Impl`, `Engine`, `Queue`, `Area`) vs peripheral suffixes (`Action`, `Dialog`, `Handler`, `Delegate`, `Provider`, `Strategy`, `Util`, `Test`, `Bundle`, `Inspection`) and non-source extensions (`.xml`, `.md`, `.json`, `.properties`).

Applied as a **small additive prior in the CE final ordering** (breaks saturation ties without overriding clear CE wins) and optionally in the **graph-file seed scoring** (to lift hubs into the CE window).
