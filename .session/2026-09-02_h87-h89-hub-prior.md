## Falsified

1. **Filename-role priors are workload-dependent and unsafe**: penalising `.xml/.yaml` destroys `config-*` queries whose targets *are* descriptors; penalising `*Provider` kills legitimate EP-implementation targets. Rejected as a general signal.
2. **Additive priors on the CE head destroy precision**: any prior that can reorder a confident CE top-1/2/3 costs hit@1 on `path_symbol` queries. Hub priors belong to the tail.
3. Seed-stage hub prior (graph-file) did not rescue the 4 seed-compression deaths (h88b/c) — those targets need a different lever.
